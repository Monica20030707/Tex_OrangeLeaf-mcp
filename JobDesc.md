Abacus.AI is the world’s first AI super assistant built on our state-of-the-art generative AI technology. We are headquartered in San Francisco, with a dynamic team spread across the world.



Role Overview: We are seeking highly motivated and experienced Sales/Business Development Representatives who are interested in becoming Enterprise Account Executives within their first 6-12 months. You will be identifying and engaging potential clients, and driving the initial stages of our sales funnel until you're consistently hitting goals and promoted into AE bootcamp where you take on the full sales cycle. If you are passionate about technology, enjoy connecting with people, and thrive in a fast-paced environment, we want to hear from you!



Key Responsibilities:

Lead Generation: Identify and qualify potential clients through research, outbound prospecting, and networking.
Outreach: Utilize sales engagement platforms, along with parallel dialers to efficiently connect with prospects.
Pipeline Management: Maintain and manage a robust pipeline of leads moving through the sales cycle, ensuring timely follow-ups and accurate record-keeping in our CRM system.
Collaboration: Work closely with the sales team to develop strategies that drive conversion.
Opportunity Development: Engage prospects in consultative discovery discussions, qualify opportunities against key criteria, and help progress deals through the sales process.


Qualifications:

Experience: 2+ years as an SDR/BDR/AE within a tech company, preferably a startup.
Bachelor’s degree in Business, Marketing, or related field is preferred but not required.
Technical Proficiency: Expertise in using sales engagement platforms like LinkedIn, Outreach, Salesloft and parallel dialers such as Orum or Nooks.
Autonomy: Proven ability to work independently, managing time and resources effectively.
Ambition: A strong desire to advance to an Enterprise Account Executive role, with a relentless drive to achieve and exceed goals.
Problem-Solving: Demonstrated ability to think creatively and implement innovative approaches to overcome challenges and meet objectives.
Resilience: Capacity to perform under pressure, maintaining focus and effectiveness in a high-paced environment.
