Problems worth solving
Here are some of the concrete problems you could work on.

Document intelligence
Classify, extract, rename, and cross-check borrower documents across applications, statements, tax returns, invoices, IDs, and financials.

Agentic workflows
Design agents that observe a deal, branch based on context, gather evidence, and preserve their reasoning.

Bank statement analysis
Separate true revenue from transfers, refunds, MCA proceeds, owner injections, and one-time deposits.

Business verification
Combine Secretary of State records, web presence, domain age, address signals, ownership clues, and industry context.

Credit memo generation
Turn analysis into clear, editable, source-backed memos that credit teams can trust.

Enterprise integrations
Push structured outputs into Salesforce, LOS/CRM systems, file stores, and lender workflows without forcing rip-and-replace.

Security and reliability
Build infrastructure that handles sensitive borrower data with the discipline financial institutions expect.

How we work
01
Start from the file, not the demo.
We care about what happens inside real borrower packages, not just polished product flows.

02
Automate the science. Preserve the art.
The goal is not to remove human judgment. It is to remove the manual work that prevents good judgment from scaling.

03
Reasoning should be visible.
In credit workflows, answers are not enough. Users need to know why the system concluded what it did.

04
Ship with evidence.
We like fast iteration, but production systems need tests, evals, observability, and customer feedback.

05
Earn trust in the details.
Security, traceability, accuracy, and workflow fit matter when your users are lenders.
