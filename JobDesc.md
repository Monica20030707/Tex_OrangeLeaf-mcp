What you’ll do:

We are on a mission to make better health easy. Every day our employees contribute to this mission and help hundreds of thousands of people around the globe improve their health. How cool is that? We are looking for passionate, smart, and collaborative people who have a desire to do something meaningful and impactful in their career.

The Associate Software Developer will act as the technical bridge between our core engineering products and our global client base. This role is perfect for an early-career developer who enjoys problem solving, debugging, and working closely with Client Success teams to ensure our platforms perform at scale. You will not just “support” the product; you will own its reliability and grow alongside it.

The primary responsibilities of this job include:

Support the Client Success team by investigating and resolving client-reported technical issues across web and mobile platforms.
Perform scenario-based testing for development tickets - designing and executing test cases that reflect real user workflows, edge cases, and failure conditions before and after releases.
Monitor, debug, and escalate issues within RESTful web services, flagging revenue-impacting problems with clarity and urgency.
Assist in eligibility file processing and system monitoring to help maintain data integrity across environments.
Coordinate seamlessly with India-based support team to ensure maximum support coverage and exceptional client experience.
Participate in design and code reviews, contributing constructive feedback and learning from senior engineers.
